What to Expect

DevFestMN is a one-day conference for the Twin Cities developer community: two tracks of talks and hands-on workshops from local practitioners and industry experts, lunch, and a room full of people who build software for a living.

  • AI & agents MCP servers, agent-to-agent protocols, and building with Google's ADK.
  • Architecture & DevOps Guardrails, API integration patterns, and secure deployment pipelines.
  • Design & product Design systems, building for new regulations, and staying human with AI.
  • Careers & craft How developers work, bill, and thrive, plus a hands-on workshop.

DevFestMN has run since 2013 and is organized by volunteers from GDG Twin Cities. Attendees range from students to seasoned professionals, and the day is built for learning from each other as much as from the stage.

GDG DevFests are community-run developer conferences held by Google Developer Groups around the world. Each one is planned by its local group, with its own speakers and topics.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is DevFestMN for?

Anyone who builds software, or wants to: developers, designers, data and ML folks, engineering leaders, and students. Sessions range from introductions to deep dives, so you'll find something at every experience level.

Is lunch included?

Yes. Your ticket includes a catered lunch, along with every talk and workshop.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are available through Eventbrite up to 14 days before the event, minus Eventbrite's fees. See the refund policy.
